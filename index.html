01 / The world of Abrek

From the mountains.
A world of our own.

Stone towers above narrow passes. Fortified villages carved into the mountains. A world shaped by the architecture, clothing, and weapons of the Caucasus, with Chechen and broader Vainakh influences.

Abrek is a fictional world with an original story, not a recreation of historical events. Its PS2-inspired, stylized low-poly art gives every silhouette a distinct character.

04 / Development

Built by hand.
Shared as it grows.

Abrek is in active development in Unity. Combat and environment prototypes give a first look at the project; animations, enemy AI, bosses, and world design continue to evolve.

The working title, systems, and scope may change. No release date, public demo, store listing, or supported platform lineup has been announced here.
